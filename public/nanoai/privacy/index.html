NanoAI Privacy Policy

Omega AI LLC · Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy explains how NanoAI, the AI assistant app made by Omega AI LLC, handles your information, including data from Google accounts you connect to it. For our website and other products, see the Omega AI privacy policy.

In short:

  • NanoAI runs on your own Windows PC. Your conversations, memory, tasks and files are stored there.
  • To answer you, NanoAI sends the content each request needs to an AI model, either through Nano Cloud (our hosted service) or to a model running on your PC.
  • Nano Cloud doesn't store what you send. Our AI provider doesn't use it to train models.
  • Google data is used only to do what you ask. It's never sold, used for ads, or used to train general-purpose AI models.

1. How NanoAI works

NanoAI is a Windows app that holds conversations, remembers what you tell it, runs tasks, and works with your apps and accounts. It stores its data in a NanoAI data folder on your PC, which it restricts to your Windows account. Speech recognition, spoken replies and voice matching run on your PC.

To generate replies, NanoAI uses one of these:

  • Nano Cloud, Omega AI's hosted AI service. NanoAI sends it the content a request needs, and Nano Cloud passes that to Microsoft Azure to produce the reply.
  • A local model you choose. With a local model, that processing stays on your PC.

3. AI processing through Nano Cloud

When you use the hosted model, each request carries what's needed to answer it. That can include:

  • your message and relevant earlier conversation;
  • saved memory;
  • screenshots of the computer NanoAI is working on;
  • results from tools, such as the contents of an email NanoAI read for you.
  • Nano Cloud stores no request content. It checks your account, counts the tokens used, and forwards the request to Microsoft Azure. Every request asks Azure not to keep the response.
  • Microsoft Azure generates the reply. Under Microsoft's data, privacy and security terms, prompts and replies aren't used to train models and aren't available to other customers or to the model's developer. Microsoft may keep a sample of flagged requests for abuse review, as those terms describe.
  • NanoAI doesn't use your content to train AI models, and neither does Omega AI.

How Google data moves

  • Signing in. Google sign-in goes through Omega AI's sign-in service at plugins.omega2ai.com. It holds NanoAI's Google app secret, exchanges the one-time authorization code for tokens and refreshes them, then returns the tokens to your PC. It doesn't store or log authorization codes or tokens.
  • Using the data. NanoAI sends requests to Google from your PC using your tokens. Google's replies come back to your PC.
  • Answering you. When you use Nano Cloud, the Google data a request needs (for example the emails you asked NanoAI to summarize) is sent through Nano Cloud to Microsoft Azure to produce the answer, as described in section 3. Nano Cloud doesn't store it.
  • On your PC. Results are saved in your conversation or task results. If automatic memory is on, or you ask NanoAI to remember something, a short fact may be saved to NanoAI's memory on your PC. You can review and delete memories in Settings.

Limited Use

NanoAI's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • We use Google user data only to provide and improve the user-facing features you use in NanoAI.
  • We transfer it only as needed to provide those features (the AI processing in section 3), for security, to comply with law, or with your consent.
  • We don't sell it, and we don't use it for advertising, retargeting or credit decisions.
  • No person at Omega AI reads it, unless you ask us to, it's needed for security or to comply with law, or it's aggregated and anonymized for internal operations.
  • NanoAI does not use Google Workspace data to develop, improve, or train generalized or non-personalized AI or machine-learning models. When it sends Google data to its AI provider to answer your request, that provider doesn't use it to train models either.

Disconnecting and deleting

  • Remove a Google plugin in NanoAI's Plugins to delete its saved tokens from your PC.
  • You can also revoke NanoAI's access at Google Account → Third-party connections.
  • Delete conversations, tasks and memories in NanoAI, or delete NanoAI's data folder to remove everything it stored on your PC.

5. Who else processes data

  • Microsoft Azure generates replies for Nano Cloud (section 3).
  • Netlify hosts Omega AI's website and the sign-in service.
  • Cloudflare carries encrypted traffic to Nano Cloud.
  • Apps you connect, such as Google, receive the requests NanoAI makes for you, under their own privacy policies.
  • Phone access, if you turn it on, connects your phone to NanoAI on your PC through your own private Tailscale network.

We don't sell personal information or share it with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Keeping and deleting data

  • Data on your PC stays until you delete it in NanoAI or delete NanoAI's data folder.
  • Nano Cloud keeps your account email and daily usage counts, not what you sent.
  • The sign-in service keeps nothing.
  • Microsoft's handling is set out in its terms linked in section 3.
  • To close a Nano Cloud account or ask about your data, email noahlee@omega2ai.com.

7. Security

  • NanoAI's connections to Nano Cloud, the sign-in service and Google use encrypted HTTPS.
  • App secrets stay on Omega AI's servers and are never shipped in NanoAI.
  • NanoAI's data folder on your PC is restricted to your Windows account.

8. Children

NanoAI isn't directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live).

9. Changes

When this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how NanoAI uses data you've already given it, we'll tell you in the app or by email first.
